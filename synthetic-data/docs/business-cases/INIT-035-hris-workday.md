1. THE PROBLEM

Three HR systems. A legacy on-premise HRIS for the original Harbourline entities, a
second instance inherited with the Kessler acquisition, and a standalone Kronos deployment
for time and attendance in the depots. Employee data does not reconcile between them.
Headcount reporting to the Board is manually assembled every month and takes four days.

We cannot answer "how many people work here" without a spreadsheet. This is embarrassing
and it is also blocking other work.

2. PROPOSAL

Consolidate to a single Workday instance. Core HCM, absence, and reporting in phase 1.
Talent and performance in phase 2 (not in this case, not funded).

Time and attendance: we are keeping Kronos and integrating. Replacing it was descoped in
January to protect the go-live date.

3. FINANCIALS

Budget                $460,000
Forecast at completion $611,000
Annual benefit        $670,000
Payback               ~2 years (approx)
Duration              7 months, Mar 2026 - Oct 2026

The forecast is 33% over budget. This is the largest percentage overrun in the HR function
and it is driven almost entirely by the data migration, which has taken roughly three times
the effort estimated. The estimate was based on the vendor's standard assumptions about
source data quality. Our source data quality is not standard.

4. BENEFIT

$670k annually, made up of:
  - Licence and support consolidation (2 systems retired)  ~$310k
  - HR admin effort reduction                              ~$240k
  - Reporting effort (the four days a month)               ~$120k

Confidence: Medium. The licence saving is contractual and certain. The effort reduction
assumes the HR operating model changes to actually remove the roles, which is a separate
initiative and is not committed.

5. WHO IS WAITING FOR US

This matters more than the business case suggests:

  - HR Shared Services Operating Model runs on Workday case management. There is no
    platform for them before we deliver. This is a hard technical dependency.
  - Manager Capability Programme wants Workday performance and org data to target its
    interventions. Softer - they could proceed with a manual list.
  - The Finance Shared Services Centre draws establishment data from us for their
    resourcing model.
  - Organisational Delayering needs reliable reporting-line and establishment data. They
    have told us the current org data "does not reconcile" and they are right.
