## INBOUND FREIGHT COST REDUCTION (INIT-023)

**What**

Renegotiate and re-tender inbound freight across the European lanes. We currently run 31
carriers on 140 lanes with no consolidated volume commitment and no lane-level cost model.
Roughly 40% of inbound moves are spot-booked at whatever rate is available on the day.

Consolidate to 8–10 carriers, commit volume, build the lane cost model, move spot to
contracted where it makes sense.

**Value**

$1.89m annually. Confidence HIGH — unusually for this portfolio, this one is well evidenced.
We ran the same exercise in the Nordics region in FY24 and achieved 11.2% against a 9% target,
and the European lane profile is comparable. The number here assumes 9.5%.

This should be treated as a quick win. It is cheap ($1,315,000), short (11 months), needs no
new technology, and the benefit is contractual once the rates are signed.

**Cost / timing**

Budget: 1,315,000
Start: Feb 2026
End: Jan 2027
Benefit starts: after the contracts are signed and the first full month of shipments runs
against new rates, so realistically Q1 FY28 for anything material.

Capex: minimal, it's basically people
Opex: [TBC — Finance to confirm split]

**Dependencies**

Not many, which is why it's a good early win.

The one thing worth noting is that the lane cost model we build here is reused by the
Distribution Network Redesign. They're modelling which depots to keep and they need
lane-level cost to do it. So there's value in us finishing before they get deep into
modelling, though it's not a hard blocker — they could use the old rates and adjust.

We do NOT depend on the WMS upgrade. Somebody put that in the dependency register and it's
wrong. Different scope entirely.

**Risks**

- FX on the non-GBP lanes could erode the saving. We're reporting at constant currency and
  Finance have agreed that.
- Carrier consolidation reduces resilience. If we go to 8 carriers and one fails we have less
  optionality. Mitigation is dual-sourcing the top 20 lanes by volume.
- Peak season capacity — committing volume in a tight market can work against us.

**Status at time of writing**

Green. 50% complete. Tracking slightly under budget (forecast $1,268,000 against $1,315,000).
