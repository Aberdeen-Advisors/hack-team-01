# Supplier Rationalisation Wave 2

**INIT-020** · Supply Chain · Sponsor: MD Distribution & Logistics · Lead: Bjorn Whitfield

---

### The opportunity

Wave 1 took the indirect supplier base from ~6,400 to ~4,100 and delivered against its target.
Wave 2 goes after the remaining tail: roughly 2,500 suppliers accounting for under 4% of
addressable spend but a disproportionate share of transaction cost, onboarding effort and
third-party risk exposure.

Target end state is approximately 1,600 suppliers.

### Value

**$3.1m per annum** — though see the caveat below, because this number is contested.

Breakdown as we present it:

| Source | Annual |
|---|---|
| Price harmonisation onto retained suppliers | ~$1.9m |
| Transaction and onboarding cost reduction | ~$0.7m |
| Rebate capture on consolidated volume | ~$0.5m |

**Caveat.** Group Finance have challenged the price harmonisation component on the basis that
some of it is already claimed by the Indirect Spend Category Reset (INIT-050). We think the
overlap is around $400–600k. This has been open since May and is not resolved. Until it is,
the honest number is somewhere between $2.5m and $3.1m and we are reporting the top of the
range, which I am not comfortable with.

### Sequence

The category strategies from the Indirect Spend Category Reset define which suppliers are on
the retained list. We cannot start negotiating consolidation without them — that is a hard
predecessor and it is the reason our mobilisation gate slipped.

The Source-to-Pay redesign matters to us in the other direction: the new vendor master and
onboarding workflow is what actually *prevents the tail growing back*. If S2P does not land,
we will rationalise 2,500 suppliers and watch the number climb again within eighteen months,
which is exactly what happened after Wave 1 in the Nordics region.

Downstream, the Third-Party & Supplier Risk Management programme is scoring the rationalised
base, not the current one. They have been told to wait for us.

### Where we actually are

Mobilisation & scope sign-off was baselined for **13 May 2026**. It was missed. Current
forecast **11 August 2026** — a 90-day slip. We are 12% complete against a plan that says we
should be considerably further on.

Budget $2,635,000. Forecast at completion **$3,535,000**. That is 34% over and it is the
worst variance in the Supply Chain function. The overrun is contractor day rates and a scope
addition (the Nordics entities were added in March after the case was approved).

Current RAG: **Red**. It has been Red since April.

### Risks

- **Licence pricing renegotiation lands above the business case** — score 25, our highest.
  Currently mitigating.
- Training materials for the new supplier onboarding process are not signed off. Critical,
  in progress, 35 days of schedule impact attributed.
- Benefit double-count with INIT-050 (see above) — this is a financial credibility risk more
  than a delivery risk, but it is the one that will get asked about at Board.

### What good looks like

By July 2027 we are down to 1,600 suppliers, the new onboarding workflow is the only route
to creating a vendor record, and Finance has signed a benefit baseline that both we and
INIT-050 agree on.
