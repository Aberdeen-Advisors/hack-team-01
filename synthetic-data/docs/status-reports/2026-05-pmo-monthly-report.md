## 3. Escalations to the Committee

### 3.1 Test environment contention — CROSS-PORTFOLIO
Four initiatives are now reporting schedule impact from contention on the shared
pre-production environment. INIT-035 (HRIS Consolidation to Workday) has attributed 50 days
of slip to it and is the worst affected. INIT-006 and INIT-039 are also affected.

The PMO recommendation is that a second environment is funded. Estimated cost $180k. This
has been raised in three consecutive reports without a decision.

**Decision required: fund the second environment, or accept the slip and re-baseline the
four affected initiatives.**

### 3.2 Benefit double-count between INIT-020 and INIT-050
Group Finance have challenged approximately $400–600k of price harmonisation benefit that
appears in both the Supplier Rationalisation Wave 2 case and the Indirect Spend Category
Reset case. Both initiatives are currently reporting the benefit in full.

Until this is resolved the portfolio benefit total is overstated by up to $600k.

**Decision required: benefit ownership ruling from Group Finance.**

### 3.3 Resource contention forecast — 2026Q4
Early modelling suggests three roles go into significant over-allocation from October:
Data Engineer, Change Manager and Supply Chain Analyst. Peak demand for Data Engineers in
2027Q1 is forecast at roughly 1.4x available supply.

This is a forecast, not yet an issue. It becomes an issue in approximately four months.

finishes in November. Forecast at completion $1,945,000 against a $1,345,000 budget, a 45%

advisory spend control initiative is over budget on advisory spend has been noted by the CFO.

**INIT-003 API Gateway & Integration Layer** — Red, 68% complete, forecast $2,435,000 against

## 7. PMO observation

The PMO would note, for the third consecutive month, that the portfolio has no integrated
plan. Each initiative maintains its own schedule in its own tool. Dependencies are recorded
in a register that is updated when someone remembers. We are not able to answer, with
confidence, the question "if initiative X slips by three months, what else moves?"

The incoming Transformation Director takes up post in August.
