## 4. Escalations

### 4.1 Test environment — STILL UNRESOLVED
Fourth consecutive escalation. No decision taken. INIT-035 has now lost an estimated
50 days. The second environment would have paid for itself twice over by now.

### 4.2 Sequencing conflict: Organisational Delayering vs Finance Shared Services
Both initiative leads have independently raised that the Finance Shared Services Centre
(INIT-031) is designing its target operating model against a span-of-control standard that
the Organisational Delayering initiative (INIT-039) does not publish until February 2027 and
does not implement until mid-2028.

INIT-031 begins design work in August 2026.

The PMO has no mechanism to resolve this. Both plans are approved. Both are in the portfolio.
They are mutually inconsistent and have been since both were approved, three months apart, by
the same Investment Committee.

**This is escalated for a sequencing decision.**

### 4.3 Cloud migration wave sequencing
Related in character. INIT-006 (Wave 2, production) is scheduled to finish in March 2027.
INIT-005 (Wave 1, non-production), on which it depends, is scheduled to finish in September
2027. Wave 1's scope grew in Q1 and its date moved. Wave 2's date did not.

The Technology function is managing this "pragmatically" by migrating lower-tier workloads
early. The PMO's view is that this is not a plan, it is a hope.

## 5. Value realisation

Benefit realised to date: **$0**.

First planned benefit checkpoint across the portfolio is INIT-001 in July 2026, and that
initiative's annual benefit is $70k — it is an enabler with negligible standalone value.

The Committee should be clear-eyed about the shape of the value curve. Under the current
plan:

| Period | Cumulative planned benefit |
|---|---|
| To end 2026 | negligible |
| 2027 H1 | small — a handful of the shorter cost initiatives |
| 2027 H2 | the curve starts |
| 2028 | the majority of run-rate value |

Nothing about this is wrong in principle — value follows delivery. But a portfolio that spends
$95m and books nothing for two years is a difficult story, and the Board has said it wants to
see value inside twelve months.

## 6. Notable movements

**INIT-035 HRIS Consolidation to Workday** — the June go-live has been achieved and the
system is live across all entities. Strong result from the team given the environment
difficulties. Moving to Green.

**INIT-054 Energy Efficiency & Utilities Programme** — paused pending a decision on the
site consolidation strategy. There is no point insulating a depot we are going to close.
Sensible pause, correctly taken.

**INIT-017 Health & Safety Digital Reporting** — paused. This is a regulatory initiative and
the PMO is uncomfortable with the pause. Recommend the Committee review.
