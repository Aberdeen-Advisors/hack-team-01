## 7. Handover note to the incoming Transformation Director

The PMO's honest assessment, offered without defensiveness:

1. **There is no integrated roadmap.** Sixty initiatives, sixty plans, four planning tools.
2. **Dependencies are recorded but not managed.** The register has around 95 entries. Nobody
   has ever run a critical path across it. At least three hard dependencies are violated by
   the current dates and have been for months.
3. **The benefit case is not credible in aggregate.** $84m of annual benefit is claimed
   against $95m of spend. Finance have signed off almost none of it. At least one benefit is
   claimed twice.
4. **Resource constraints are visible four months out and unmanaged.**
5. **Nothing has been cancelled.** In two years the portfolio has grown from 34 initiatives
   to 60. Nothing has ever been stopped.

The question the PMO cannot answer, and which we are asked most often, is: *if we could only
do thirty of these, which thirty?*
