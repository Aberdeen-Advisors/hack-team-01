## 2. Portfolio performance — May report

The PMO Lead presented. Discussion focused on the Red count (10) and the absence of any
realised benefit after eighteen months.

The **Group CFO** observed that the portfolio has consumed $12.4m and returned nothing, and
asked when the Committee should expect to see the first validated saving. The PMO Lead
responded that under the current plan the first material benefit lands in Q1 FY28.

The CFO's response is minuted at his request: *"Then we are not running a transformation
programme, we are running a capital expenditure programme with a transformation
vocabulary."*

**No decision taken.** The Chair asked that the incoming Transformation Director be given
this as a first priority.

## 3. Sequencing conflict — Organisational Delayering (INIT-039)

The **Group HR Director** raised, for the third time, that INIT-039 is a prerequisite for at
least three other initiatives and that those initiatives are not sequenced behind it.

Specifically:
- INIT-031 Finance Shared Services Centre — designing from August 2026
- INIT-038 Shared Services HR Operating Model — starting July 2026
- INIT-013 Contact Centre Operating Model Redesign — softer dependency

INIT-039 does not publish a target structure until February 2027 and does not complete
implementation until June 2028.

The **Group Financial Controller** (present for this item) argued that the Finance SSC cannot
wait until 2028, that the saving is needed in the FY28 plan, and that the SSC can be designed
against an *assumed* span-of-control standard and adjusted later.

The **Group HR Director** disagreed, stating that "designed and adjusted later" means designed
twice and paid for twice, and that the adjustment in question is the entire supervisory layer
of the centre.

The **Chair** noted that both positions had been stated at the previous two meetings without
resolution and that the Committee was now going in circles.

**DECISION:** None. The item is deferred to the July meeting with a request that the PMO
produce "an integrated view of the dependency". The PMO Lead noted that producing such a view
is not currently possible with the tooling available.

> *Secretariat note: this item has now been deferred three times. It was first raised in the
> April meeting.*

## 4. Benefit double-count — INIT-020 / INIT-050

The Group Financial Controller presented Finance's analysis. Approximately $400–600k of price
harmonisation benefit appears in both the Supplier Rationalisation Wave 2 case and the
Indirect Spend Category Reset case.

**DECISION (D-2606-01):** Group Finance will make a binding ruling on benefit ownership by
31 July 2026. Neither initiative may report the disputed benefit until the ruling is made.
Owner: Group Financial Controller.

*(Status at time of publication: not delivered.)*

## 5. INIT-054 Energy Efficiency & Utilities Programme — pause request

The Group CFO requested a pause pending the depot consolidation decision (INIT-014), on the
grounds that energy efficiency capital should not be deployed into sites that may close.

**DECISION (D-2606-02):** INIT-054 paused with immediate effect. To be reviewed once
INIT-014 reaches a site-level decision. Owner: Group CFO.

The Committee noted that INIT-054 is 67% complete and that pausing at this point strands the
investment made to date. The Chair accepted this as the lesser cost.

## 6. Test environment contention

The **CIO** supported. The **Group CFO** asked whether the $180k could come from within the
existing Technology envelope rather than as new money. The CIO stated that the Technology
envelope is already forecasting over.

**DECISION:** Deferred. The Chair asked for a paper quantifying the cost of *not* doing it.

> *Secretariat note: the PMO estimates the slip already attributable to environment contention
