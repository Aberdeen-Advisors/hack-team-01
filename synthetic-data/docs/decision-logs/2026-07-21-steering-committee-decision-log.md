## Decisions taken

### D-2707-01 — INIT-011 Field Service Route Optimisation: DEFERRED
**Decision:** INIT-011 is deferred, not paused. Work stops. The initiative is removed from the
FY27 delivery plan and will be reconsidered in the FY28 planning round.

**Rationale:** the initiative depends on telematics data from INIT-019, which is itself only
32% complete and not producing usable location data. Proceeding now means the optimisation
team builds against synthetic data.

**Owner:** Group COO
**Note:** the tracker currently records this as "Paused". It should read "Deferred". These are
different things — a pause implies resumption on a known trigger; a deferral does not. The PMO
was asked to correct the record.

*(Status: not corrected at time of publication. The tracker still says Paused.)*

### D-2707-02 — INIT-020 Supplier Rationalisation Wave 2: RAG change to Amber
**Decision:** INIT-020 moves from Red to Amber for the July reporting cycle on the basis of an
agreed recovery plan.

**Rationale (as stated by the sponsor, MD Distribution & Logistics):** the mobilisation slip is
understood, a recovery plan is agreed, and the initiative is "back on track".

**Dissent recorded:** the initiative lead (Bjorn Whitfield) did not agree with the change and
asked for his objection to be minuted. His position: the initiative is 12% complete against a
plan that should have it considerably further on, is forecasting 34% over budget, and has a
critical issue open on training material sign-off. He considers Amber to be a misrepresentation.

**Chair's ruling:** the sponsor owns the RAG. Change stands. The lead's objection is minuted.

> *Secretariat note: the Committee may wish to consider whether a RAG owned by the sponsor
> rather than the delivery lead is a control or a comfort.*

### D-2707-03 — Test environment: APPROVED
**Decision:** $180k approved for a second pre-production environment. Funded from central
contingency.

**Rationale:** the Chair accepted the PMO's estimate of ~90 days of aggregate slip already
incurred. Five escalations was considered sufficient.

**Owner:** CIO. **Target:** environment available by end September 2026.

### D-2707-04 — INIT-017 Health & Safety Digital Reporting: UNPAUSE DIRECTED
**Decision:** the Committee directs that INIT-017 be restarted. A regulatory initiative may not
be paused without Committee approval, and no such approval was sought.

**Owner:** Group COO, with Chief Risk Officer oversight.

*(Status at time of publication: the initiative remains Paused in the structured tracker and
is reported as Red. The July PMO report describes it as Green and on schedule. The PMO has
been asked which of these is correct and has not yet responded.)*

### Organisational Delayering sequencing conflict — DEFERRED (4th time)
The Chair proposed that the incoming Transformation Director resolve this in their first month
rather than the Committee attempt it again.

The **Group Financial Controller** objected on the grounds that INIT-031 begins design work in
August and will have designed against the old structure before any decision is made. The
**Group HR Director** agreed with the objection but not with the proposed solution.

The item was deferred. Both parties agreed the deferral was unsatisfactory.

**The Chair's closing remark is minuted at the request of the Group HR Director:**
*"We have now spent four meetings agreeing that this is a problem and no meetings deciding
what to do about it. I am not confident a fifth would be different."*
